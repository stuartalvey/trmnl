## Purpose and operator

Alvey TRMNL is a private, noncommercial household dashboard operated by **Stu Alvey**. It uses information that participating adults choose to authorize through Oura to display selected daily measurements and recent trends on a household TRMNL device. It is an independent project, not an Oura or TRMNL product.

Contact: participating household members can contact Stu Alvey directly through their existing private contact method. Do not post health information or credentials in public GitHub issues.

Before connecting, review the permissions requested by Oura and the app’s [Privacy Policy](oura-privacy.md). You may decline a permission, disconnect or withdraw consent. A missing permission may prevent the corresponding dashboard feature from working. Using the dashboard does not authorize additional data categories, recipients or purposes.

## Display limits and privacy

The screen can be seen by anyone in its physical location and may retain its last image while offline or unpowered. Do not place it where information will be exposed to people you have not agreed may see it. Do not publish another person’s dashboard, records or screenshots without that person’s express permission.

Do not place account passwords, client secrets, access tokens, refresh tokens or personal health records in the public project repository or public issue reports.
